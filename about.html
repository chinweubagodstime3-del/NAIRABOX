About Us

Nairabox is Nigeria’s leading digital lifestyle platform, revolutionizing how people discover and book experiences. Whether you’re catching a movie, attending
a concert, or exploring the hottest live events, Nairabox makes access simple, fast and secure.

At the heart of our platform is a commitment to innovation. We leverage technology to deliver seamless ticketing for cinema, concerts, and more directly to
your device. Our digital first approach ensures you never miss out on the moments that matter, while offering a smooth and intuitive booking experience

In 2025, Nairabox became part of the Wakanow Group, merging entertainment and travel in a strategic partnership that reflects our long-term vision: to
create a unified ecosystem where lifestyle, culture, and experiences are just a click away.

Vision

To create Africa’s most unified digital ecosystem where lifestyle, culture,
entertainment, and travel come together seamlessly, empowering people to
experience the moments that matter with ease.

Misson

To revolutionize how people discover, book, and enjoy experiences by
leveraging innovation and technology to deliver fast, secure, and intuitive
access to movies, events, and entertainment anytime, anywhere.
