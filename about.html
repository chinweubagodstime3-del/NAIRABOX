About Us

Nairabox is Nigeria’s leading digital lifestyle platform, revolutionizing how people discover and book experiences. Whether you’re catching a movie, attending a concert, or exploring the hottest live events, Nairabox makes access simple, fast and secure.

At the heart of our platform is a commitment to innovation. We leverage technology to deliver seamless ticketing for cinema, concerts, and more directly to your device. Our digital-first approach ensures you never miss out on the moments that matter, while offering a smooth and intuitive booking experience.

In 2025, Nairabox became part of the Wakanow Group, merging entertainment and travel in a strategic partnership that reflects our long-term vision: to create a unified ecosystem where lifestyle, culture, and experiences are just a click away.

Our Vision
